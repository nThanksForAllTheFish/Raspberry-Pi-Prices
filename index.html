About the projection

The dotted line carries TrendForce's 30 September forecast of +10–15% for Q4 2026 conventional DRAM contract prices onto the spot index at roughly +4% per month, reaching about 1,030 in November and 1,070 in December. The shaded range runs from a flat case (spot has not moved since mid-September, and one analyst calls Q4 at +0–5%) up to the high end of the PC DRAM forecast (+13–18%). Nobody publishes November- or December-specific numbers; these are derived from quarterly ranges. Relief is not in any forecast before late 2027: Nanya's new fab starts wafers in H2 2027, Micron's Virginia restart only quadruples a 7% slice of its output by 2027, and Counterpoint still sees no correction before H2 2027.

Structural Drivers

Supply Contraction

Samsung, Micron, SK Hynix all phasing out DDR4/LPDDR4 to reallocate fab capacity to HBM and DDR5; Samsung's LPDDR4 lines convert after Q1 2027

AI Demand

HBM for AI accelerators commands 3× the wafer capacity vs commodity DRAM; hyperscalers absorbing supply; PC OEMs now buying ahead of expected 2027 tightness

CXMT Pivot

China's dominant DDR4 supplier shifted to DDR5 in Q1 2025 and is now in G5-node LPDDR5X production, removing the main source of cheap legacy supply
